What it does

Practice that adapts to the child

Every paper is built on the spot from a bank of over 4,000 vetted questions. No two children get the same practice, and the app gets sharper the more they use it.

Finds weak spots

It spots the exact topics a child keeps getting wrong and quietly serves more of those next time.

Short by design

Papers take about ten minutes. Little and often beats long, rare sessions - and children stick with it.

Clear progress

Parents see a simple score, a progress graph, and which topics are improving - in plain language.

Keeps it fun

Streaks, rewards and a paper companion that grows as the child masters more topics keep motivation up.

Who it's for
