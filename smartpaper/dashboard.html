How these numbers are worked out
  • Practice papers are papers a pupil has completed. The short warm-up quiz at sign-up is not counted.
  • Score is SmartPaper's overall accuracy figure, the same one parents see in the app. It starts cautiously (a single paper can't push it very high or very low) and settles to the pupil's true accuracy as they complete more papers. It is only shown once a pupil has completed a practice paper.
  • Topic accuracy is a pupil's recent accuracy in that topic, and only counts a topic once they have answered at least 3 questions in it. Class figures need at least 2 pupils with data.
  • App average is the anonymised average of all SmartPaper children in the same year group, so a Year 3 class is compared with Year 3, not with everyone.
  • Streak only shows while it is still running (weekends don't break it).
  • Struggling = score below 55 after at least 3 practice papers. Gone quiet = otherwise fine, but no practice for 7+ days.
  • Only families who joined the school pilot (and agreed to share) appear here.
