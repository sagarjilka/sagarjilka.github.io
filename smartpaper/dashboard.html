How these numbers are worked out
  • Practice paper = a paper a pupil has finished. The short warm-up quiz at sign-up is never counted.
  • Score (out of 100) is SmartPaper's overall accuracy figure, the same one parents see. It starts cautiously, so one paper can't push it very high or low, and settles to the pupil's true accuracy as they practise. Only shown once a pupil has finished a practice paper.
  • The six groups, in this order of priority: Needs support = score under 55 after at least 3 papers. Gone quiet = has practised, but nothing for 7+ days. Not started = no practice paper yet. Getting started = fewer than 3 papers, too early to judge. Doing brilliantly = score 75+ and practising within the last 7 days. On track = everyone else.
  • Practised this week = finished a practice paper in the last 7 days.
  • Topic accuracy is each pupil's recent accuracy, only counted once they have answered at least 3 questions in that topic. A class figure needs at least 2 pupils. The tick is the average of all SmartPaper children in the same year group.
  • Trend compares a pupil's latest 3 papers with the 3 before. It only shows a direction once they have 4+ papers, and ignores changes under 5 points.
  • Only families who joined the school pilot, and agreed to share, appear here. Families who joined later may simply not have started yet.
